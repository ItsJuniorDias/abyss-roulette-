Aurum Club — Golden Lion favicon

Canonical vector: public/favicon.svg
A front-facing geometric lion, champagne-gold mane, ivory-gold face and
an emerald-black rounded tile. No text; the silhouette stays legible at 16 px.

Colors: #0c211b background, #f9e3aa highlight, #d8b66c gold, #9d7737 shadow.

Browser assets:
  public/favicon.svg — scalable 64x64 vector
  public/favicon.ico — 16x16, 32x32 and 48x48 raster entries
  public/favicon-32x32.png — transparent-corner PNG fallback
  public/apple-touch-icon.png — opaque 180x180 iPhone home-screen icon

All assets are linked in index.html. The existing decorative crown at
public/assets/luxury/crest.svg remains a separate asset.
Raster exports were rendered from the canonical SVG using Sharp, with ICO
sizes assembled using Pillow. Regenerate the exports if the vector changes.
